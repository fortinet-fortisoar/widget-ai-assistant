# Installation

> [!NOTE]  
> The **Fortinet AI Assistant** widget installs with the [*FortiAI* solution pack](https://github.com/fortinet-fortisoar/solution-pack-fortinet-advisor/blob/develop/docs/setup.md#installation) and hence may not require separate installation.

1. Click **Content Hub** > **Discover**.

2. Search **AI Assistant** in the list of widgets that appears. 

3. Click the **AI Assistant** widget card.

# Configuration

> [!NOTE]  
> The **Fortinet AI Assistant** is automatically configured after a successful installation of the [*FortiAI* solution pack](https://github.com/fortinet-fortisoar/solution-pack-fortinet-advisor/blob/develop/docs/setup.md#configuration) and hence does not require an independent configuration.
