# What's New

- The AI Assistant can understand and respond to context sensitive conversations and perform given SOC tasks.
    - With bot's enhanced understanding of natural language, the modes &mdash; Playbook Blocks and Conversation Mode &mdash; have become redundant and hence were removed. 
    - Sensitive data is being actively masked for all interactions with the API, with bot's increased access to various modules, maintaining privacy is paramount.
    - The bot could always generate playbooks for all applicable modules; and now with access to create and update records, automation through playbooks became a whole lot interesting!
    - Through enhanced comprehension, the bot can also navigate to modules, its records, and playbook logs; in addition to alert investigation, remediation, escalation to incident and even setting up war rooms.
    - For increased understanding, bot can now ask questions if it needs more information or context to perform a task. For example, in response to block an indicator, it can ask for the indicator's value, type, and reason for blocking.

- The past conversations remain in chatbot window and help provide context to the bot, even after the user has logged out.
    - These conversations are loaded into the window on login.

- With improved validations, the bot can now check if a connector is installed and configured &ndash; among multitude of other validations.
